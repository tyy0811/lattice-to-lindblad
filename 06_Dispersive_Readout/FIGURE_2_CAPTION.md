# Figure 2 — Active-Loss Budget and Calibration Sensitivity

## Caption

**Figure 2.** Assignment infidelity at REFERENCE_DEVICE, 500 ns readout, F_full ≈ 0.990, n_shots = 10⁵. **Panel A — Active-loss decomposition.** The ideal readout floor (1 − F_ideal ≈ 7.5 × 10⁻³) is the finite-overlap discrimination cost that remains after all four decoherence channels are disabled — i.e., the SNR limit of dispersive readout at this pulse and integration window without any decoherence. The four active-loss bars show the marginal cost of each channel at baseline, sorted by magnitude: Purcell is the dominant contribution at g/Δ ≈ 0.044; T1, thermal, and pure dephasing are all at the shot-noise floor (~10⁻³) and are presented with their uncertainties. Bars appear for channels above 1σ; channels consistent with zero render as points with error bars. The grey "residual" point is R_active ≡ (F_ideal − F_full) − Σ ΔF_c and measures cross-channel interaction. **The residual is consistent with zero within the shot-noise propagation of the identity** (|R| ≲ σ_R); it is rendered as a point-with-errorbar, and active-loss points for which |ΔF_c| < σ_ΔF are annotated "~0" rather than with their signed numeric value, so that the figure does not invite the false reading "this channel improves fidelity". Signed values are preserved in `fig2_data.yaml`. **Panel B — Calibration sensitivity.** F loss under ±5 % drive-amplitude and ±κ/4 drive-detuning perturbations about the calibrated operating point. Detuning sensitivity is the largest calibration lever at this operating point, though the estimate is asymmetry- and shot-noise-limited — the error bar extending to ~2× the central value reflects this, not a 100 % uncertainty on the underlying physics. Panel B bars answer a different question (robustness under named perturbations) and have their own y-axis; they are **not** summable with Panel A and do not contribute to the active-loss residual identity.

## Methods note

**Methods note (Figure 2).** The decomposition is local to the reference operating point (ε₀ calibrated to F_full ≈ 0.99 on the low-amplitude branch, 500 ns pulse, 50–500 ns integration window) and is computed within the scope of the 2nd-order Schrieffer-Wolff dispersive-frame Hamiltonian. Two physics boundaries are relevant: the dispersive approximation itself is validated by unit test V2 to ≤ 2 % at REFERENCE (fidelity residual O(10⁻⁴), below bar-visibility); measurement-induced ionization (Shillito 2022) requires intra-resonator photon count n̄ > n_crit, with the reference operating point at n̄/n_crit ≈ 0.062, well below onset. Residual |1⟩→|2⟩ occupation P(|2⟩) ≈ 3 × 10⁻⁴ is entirely thermal and is attributed to the thermal channel. The operating point ε₀ is calibrated analytically from the dispersive-regime steady-state SNR formula (§2.3) and cross-verified against simulation within shot-noise tolerance. All 14 simulations share a single F_full baseline sample so per-channel ΔFs and the residual reference the same anchor (amendment 9a); ΔF is stored signed (amendment 9b); calibration-sensitivity uncertainty combines ± asymmetry with shot-noise SE of the mean-of-absolute estimator in quadrature (amendment 9c). **Caveats.** (i) This is a local operating-point decomposition; contributions change if ε₀, readout duration, or device parameters are moved. (ii) Calibration-sensitivity bars are not additive with active-loss bars and do not enter the ΔF + R identity. (iii) Several active-loss channels (pure dephasing, thermal, and at times T1 at the reference T1 = 30 μs, 500 ns readout) sit at or below the shot-noise floor; those bars are rendered as points with error bars consistent with zero rather than as filled negative bars, with signed values preserved in `fig2_data.yaml` for downstream analysis.
